# Kosta's Weekly Stutter Coach Plan

**30 minutes per day · 7 days · Mix of drills, conversation, breathing, and rest**

Use the **Weekly Plan** panel in the app sidebar to check off each day and open **Weekly Performance** for your auto-generated scorecard.

---

## Day 1 — Foundation Monday

**Focus:** Breathing, acceptance, gentle onsets, first small talk

| Min | Activity | What to do |
|-----|----------|------------|
| 0–3 | Breathing | 4-count inhale → 2-count hold → 6-count exhale. Repeat 3 cycles. Shoulders down. |
| 3–5 | Acceptance | Say aloud: *"I allow my voice to be imperfect. Blocks are information, not failure."* |
| 5–10 | Introduction | Both prompts in the app. Record tension after each. Establish your baseline score. |
| 10–22 | Gentle Onset | All 5 prompts. Retry any score under 60. Whisper-grow into each first sound. |
| 22–28 | Small Talk | Daily Chat — Round 1. Short answers only. Let the coach lead. |
| 28–30 | Reflect | Note one word that felt easy and one that was hard. Rate overall tension. |

**App exercises:** Introduction → Gentle Onset → Small Talk (Daily Chat)

---

## Day 2 — Consonant Tuesday

**Focus:** Hard consonants + meeting-style conversation

| Min | Activity | What to do |
|-----|----------|------------|
| 0–3 | Breathing | Box breathing: in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. Two rounds. |
| 3–8 | Gentle Onset warm-up | First 3 prompts only — light contact, no pressure. |
| 8–18 | Hard Consonants | All 5 prompts including "Kosta" and "presentation". Break long words into chunks. |
| 18–26 | Small Talk | Meeting Intro scenario. Practice name + role + one-sentence update. |
| 26–30 | Your Words | Drill "Kosta" and one challenge word you added. Gentle K every time. |

**App exercises:** Gentle Onset → Hard Consonants → Small Talk (Meeting Intro) → Your Words

---

## Day 3 — Pace Wednesday

**Focus:** Slow reading + longer conversation round

| Min | Activity | What to do |
|-----|----------|------------|
| 0–5 | Breathing + acceptance | 3 deep breaths, then: *"I speak at my own pace. Silence is my friend."* |
| 5–15 | Slow Reading | All 3 prompts at half speed. Pause at every comma and period. |
| 15–25 | Small Talk | Daily Chat — Round 2 (longer answers). Stay under 10 words per sentence if blocks appear. |
| 25–30 | Reflect | Compare today's avg score to Day 1. Did slower reading help tension? |

**App exercises:** Slow Reading → Small Talk (Daily Chat, Round 2)

---

## Day 4 — Recovery Thursday *(light day)*

**Focus:** Low pressure, acceptance, rest

| Min | Activity | What to do |
|-----|----------|------------|
| 0–5 | Gentle breathing | No targets. Just notice belly rise and fall. No scoring. |
| 5–15 | Slow Reading (light) | Only 1–2 prompts. If you block, pause, breathe, continue — no retry pressure. |
| 15–22 | Small Talk (optional) | 2 turns max in Daily Chat. Skip if tired — mark day complete anyway. |
| 22–26 | Acceptance affirmations | Speak slowly: *"I am more than my fluency."* *"Showing up is the win."* |
| 26–30 | Rest | Close the app. Stretch jaw and neck. Hydrate. |

**App exercises:** Slow Reading → Small Talk (optional, 2 turns)

---

## Day 5 — Power Friday

**Focus:** Hardest sounds + mixed drills

| Min | Activity | What to do |
|-----|----------|------------|
| 0–4 | Breathing | Power breath: sharp inhale through nose, long slow exhale through lips. |
| 4–14 | Hard Consonants + Your Words | Alternate plosive prompts with challenge words. 2 passes on weakest word. |
| 14–24 | Gentle Onset | Full set. Focus on words that scored lowest earlier this week. |
| 24–30 | Small Talk | Full Daily Chat round. Aim for calm, not perfect scores. |

**App exercises:** Hard Consonants → Your Words → Gentle Onset → Small Talk

---

## Day 6 — Integration Saturday

**Focus:** Combine reading, intro, and real-world phrases

| Min | Activity | What to do |
|-----|----------|------------|
| 0–3 | Breathing | 3 cycles. Set intention: *"Today I integrate what I practiced."* |
| 3–12 | Slow Reading | All prompts. Imagine reading to a friend, not performing. |
| 12–22 | Introduction + Meeting Intro | Baseline prompts, then Meeting Intro scenario end-to-end. |
| 22–30 | Challenge words | Every word in your list. Add one new word if you hit 80+ on all. |

**App exercises:** Slow Reading → Introduction → Small Talk (Meeting Intro) → Your Words

---

## Day 7 — Review Sunday

**Focus:** Week review, celebration, evaluation

| Min | Activity | What to do |
|-----|----------|------------|
| 0–5 | Breathing + acceptance | *"I completed a week of practice. That matters more than any score."* |
| 5–15 | Weakest exercise | Open **Weekly Performance** — drill whichever exercise scored lowest this week. |
| 15–25 | Small Talk finale | One round Daily Chat + one round Meeting Intro. Celebrate finishing. |
| 25–30 | Weekly evaluation | Read your auto-generated week summary. Check all 7 days in the sidebar. |

**App exercises:** (auto-suggested weakest) → Small Talk (both scenarios)

## How to track progress in the app

1. Open **http://127.0.0.1:8787** in Chrome.
2. Use the **Kosta's Week** sidebar panel — today's plan is highlighted.
3. After each 30-minute session, check off that day.
4. Scroll to **Weekly Performance** for:
   - Sessions, average score, tension trend
   - Best and toughest day
   - Day-by-day bar chart
   - End-of-week evaluation summary (from your local history)

Scores and tension ratings are saved automatically every time you complete a prompt or conversation round.

---

## Principles behind this plan

- **Gentle onset + slow rate** build motor control without fighting blocks.
- **Small Talk** transfers drills into real conversation pressure.
- **Breathing + acceptance** reduce secondary tension (the fight that makes stuttering worse).
- **Day 4 rest** prevents burnout — consistency beats intensity.
- **Day 7 review** closes the feedback loop so next week targets real weak spots.

Pair this with a speech-language pathologist for clinical guidance. This app is practice support, not a replacement for therapy.
