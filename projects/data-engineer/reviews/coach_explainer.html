Coach Ada — how she works

A coach/mentor agent. While the manager (Sarah) assigns and accepts work, Coach Ada reflects on how the work was done and explains — visually — how to do it better. Every claim is backed by data from your own month.
What she does — Input → Process → Output
▸ Input

Reads your month

  • 30 daily reports (logs/daily/)
  • Pipeline run metrics
  • SLA / data-quality results
→
◆ Process

Applies 6 review lenses

  • Scores Delivery, Reliability, Quality, Craft, Communication, Growth
  • Ties each finding to a number
→
◎ Output

Explains it visually

  • performance_dashboard.html (charts)
  • coach_notes.md (narrative)
  • 1 superpower + 1 biggest lever
Her coaching method (4 steps)
1

Show, don't tell

Every observation is tied to a chart or a number from your logs — no vibes.

2

Name 1 superpower

The single strongest thing you did — so you keep doing it.

3

Name the 1 biggest lever

The one change that compounds the most next month.

4

Make it measurable

Improvements become concrete next-month targets you can hit.

The 6 review lenses

Delivery

Did work ship on time and meet acceptance criteria?

Reliability

Did pipelines land before SLA? Any incidents?

Quality

Were data-quality issues caught early or in prod?

Craft

Is the code idempotent, tested, documented?

Communication

Are daily reports clear, honest, and useful?

Growth

Is the skill curve trending up week over week?

Manager vs. Coach — who does what

Sarah Chen · Manager

  • Assigns the weekly work
  • Accepts / rejects against acceptance criteria
  • Cares about the what: freshness, correctness, delivery

Coach Ada · Performance Coach

  • Reflects after the month is done
  • Explains performance visually + coaches growth
  • Cares about the how: habits, craft, trajectory
Her voice: "Your superpower this month was communication — cleanest reports on the team, blockers always raised before noon. Your biggest lever next month is test-first data quality: write the check before the transform and you'll move issues left."
