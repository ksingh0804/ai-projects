Harbor School Gym — Division 05 Structural Steel
Project: Harbor School Gym Addition
Section: 05 12 00

Lead time
Structural steel lead time is 14 to 16 weeks after approved shop drawings.
The clock starts on the day the engineer returns the shop drawings marked Approved.

Mill certificates
Mill certificates are required before any steel is erected.
Steel without a mill certificate stays in the laydown yard.

Bolts
Primary connections use ASTM A325 bolts.
ASTM A307 bolts are not allowed on primary connections.
Anchor bolts are submitted 4 weeks before the steel delivery date.

Shop drawings
No mill order is placed before shop drawings are approved.
