Site Logistics Plan — Harbor School Gym Addition

Gate hours
Deliveries enter from 07:00 to 15:30 on weekdays.
No deliveries during school pickup, 14:30 to 15:30.

Gates
Concrete trucks use Gate 2.
Steel trucks use Gate 1.

Laydown
Laydown Zone B is reserved for structural steel.
Rebar is stored on dunnage in Laydown Zone A, off the ground.

Weather and deliveries
If the concrete pour is delayed for weather or for an open RFI, the concrete truck is turned away at Gate 2.
Do not stage a concrete truck on the street.
