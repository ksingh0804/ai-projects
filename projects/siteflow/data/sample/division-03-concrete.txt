Harbor School Gym — Division 03 Cast-in-Place Concrete
Project: Harbor School Gym Addition
Section: 03 30 00

Curing
Keep cast-in-place concrete continuously moist for 7 days after placement.
Do not let the surface dry during those 7 days.

Strength
The slab on grade shall reach 4,000 psi at 28 days.
Test cylinders are taken from the same truck as the slab pour.

Weather limits
Do not place concrete when the air temperature is below 40 F.
Do not place concrete when rain is forecast within 4 hours of the pour.
If the temperature is below 40 F, delay the pour until the next approved weather window.

Foundation slab
The foundation pour is the slab on grade at grid lines A to D.
Vapor barrier seams must be lapped 6 inches and taped before the pour.
