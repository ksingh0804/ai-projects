# Bay Area junior interviews, 2026

Research synthesis used for the [Career Launch](../projects/career-launch.md) playbook. Public articles were read for question patterns. Full articles were not copied into `raw/`.

## What the loops separate

- **Recruiter screen (20–30 min):** clarity, motivation, authorization, location, timeline, pay. Not a coding interview.
- **Interviewer loop:** project depth, SQL or coding, a design or experiment, and a behavioral story with a real limit.

Role emphasis in 2026 writeups:

- Junior SWE: arrays, hash maps, light trees, small design, narrated coding.
- Applied AI: RAG, tools, evals, latency, cost. Refusal and measurement beat a transformer recital.
- Data engineer: window functions, idempotent loads, schema change, late data. Ask volume and latency before naming a warehouse.
- Data scientist: baselines under imbalance, experiment design, metric-drop diagnosis.

## Compensation anchors (not offers)

Levels.fyi, San Francisco Bay Area, total compensation (base + annualized equity + bonus):

- Entry-level software engineer median **$195,400** (middle half about $161k–$225k), updated 12 September 2026.
- Entry-level data scientist median **$170,900** (middle half about $136.5k–$199.5k), updated 18 June 2026.
- Google L3 SWE about **$220k** median; Meta E3 about **$189k** median.

These medians are big-tech heavy. The playbook tells the reader to ask for the req’s band and to use roughly $150k–$190k total as a junior product-company talking range.

Data source: Levels.fyi (https://www.levels.fyi).
